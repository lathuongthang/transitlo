Project Overview

The Vietnam Economic & Defence Products Exhibition Area was developed as a national-level exhibition space for VIDEX 2024.

The project integrates exhibition display, brand communication and visitor experience, creating a professional platform for Vietnam’s defence-economic enterprises.

越南国防经济产品展示区是 VIDEX 2024 国家级展览空间项目，通过展览展示、品牌传播与空间体验，构建越南企业国际交流展示平台。
