Concept Design

A quiet domestic landscape between inside and outside.

The project removes the conventional boundary between living room, courtyard and pool. Deep eaves, sliding glass and a continuous stone floor create a sequence of shaded, semi-outdoor rooms rather than a single enclosed interior.

项目弱化客厅、庭院与泳池之间的传统边界，通过深檐、极窄边玻璃推拉门与连续岩板地面，形成一系列具有遮蔽感的半室外生活空间，而非单一封闭室内。

Dự án làm mờ ranh giới truyền thống giữa phòng khách, sân trong và hồ bơi. Mái hiên sâu, cửa kính trượt khung mảnh và bề mặt đá liên tục tạo nên chuỗi không gian sống bán ngoài trời yên tĩnh.

Courtyard as the centre

Every major room returns to the water courtyard, making landscape part of everyday circulation.

Layered privacy

Glass, timber screens, planting and deep shadows form multiple levels of visual protection.

03

Material restraint

A limited palette of stone, concrete, timber and dark metal keeps the atmosphere calm and durable.
